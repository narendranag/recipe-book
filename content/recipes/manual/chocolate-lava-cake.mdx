ingredients:
  - "200g dark chocolate (70% cocoa), chopped"
  - "100g unsalted butter, plus extra for greasing"
  - "2 whole eggs"
  - "2 egg yolks"
  - "60g sugar"
  - "2 tbsp all-purpose flour"
  - "Pinch of salt"
  - "Cocoa powder for dusting"
  - "Vanilla ice cream for serving"
instructions:
  - "Preheat oven to 220\u00b0C (425\u00b0F). Butter four ramekins generously and dust with cocoa powder."
  - "Melt chocolate and butter together in a double boiler or microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring between each. Let cool slightly."
  - "Whisk eggs, egg yolks, and sugar together until thick and pale, about 2-3 minutes."
  - "Fold the melted chocolate mixture into the egg mixture gently."
  - "Sift in flour and salt. Fold until just combined — do not overmix."
  - "Divide batter evenly among the prepared ramekins."
  - "Bake for exactly 12 minutes. The edges should be firm but the center should be jiggly."
  - "Let cool for 1 minute, then run a knife around the edges. Invert onto plates."
  - "Serve immediately with a scoop of vanilla ice cream."
notes: "The key is timing — even 1 minute too long in the oven will set the center. You can prepare the batter ahead and refrigerate; add 1-2 minutes to baking time if baking from cold."
