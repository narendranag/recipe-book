ingredients:
  - "1 cup toor dal (split pigeon peas), washed"
  - "3 cups water"
  - "1/2 tsp turmeric powder"
  - "Salt to taste"
  - "2 tbsp ghee"
  - "1 tsp cumin seeds"
  - "1 tsp mustard seeds"
  - "2 dried red chilies"
  - "1 large onion, finely chopped"
  - "2 tomatoes, chopped"
  - "3 cloves garlic, sliced"
  - "1 inch ginger, julienned"
  - "1/2 tsp red chili powder"
  - "1 tsp coriander powder"
  - "Fresh cilantro for garnish"
  - "Squeeze of lemon juice"
instructions:
  - "Wash dal thoroughly until water runs clear. Add to a pot with 3 cups water, turmeric, and salt."
  - "Cook dal until soft and mushy, about 20-25 minutes. Mash lightly with a spoon for a creamy texture."
  - "For the tadka (tempering): Heat ghee in a small pan until shimmering."
  - "Add cumin seeds and mustard seeds. Let them splutter."
  - "Add dried red chilies, garlic, and ginger. Fry for 30 seconds until golden."
  - "Add onions and cook until translucent, about 3-4 minutes."
  - "Add tomatoes, chili powder, and coriander powder. Cook until tomatoes break down."
  - "Pour the sizzling tadka over the cooked dal. Stir well."
  - "Garnish with fresh cilantro and a squeeze of lemon. Serve with steamed rice or roti."
notes: "For a richer version, use a mix of toor dal and moong dal. The dal thickens as it cools — add hot water when reheating to adjust consistency."
