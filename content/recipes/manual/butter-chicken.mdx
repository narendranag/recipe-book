ingredients:
  - "500g chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces"
  - "1 cup plain yogurt"
  - "2 tbsp lemon juice"
  - "2 tsp turmeric powder"
  - "2 tsp garam masala"
  - "2 tsp red chili powder"
  - "1 tsp cumin powder"
  - "Salt to taste"
  - "3 tbsp butter"
  - "1 tbsp oil"
  - "1 large onion, finely chopped"
  - "4 cloves garlic, minced"
  - "1 inch ginger, grated"
  - "400g canned tomatoes, pureed"
  - "1 cup heavy cream"
  - "1 tsp sugar"
  - "2 tbsp kasuri methi (dried fenugreek leaves)"
  - "Fresh cilantro for garnish"
instructions:
  - "Marinate chicken with yogurt, lemon juice, turmeric, garam masala, chili powder, cumin, and salt. Refrigerate for at least 30 minutes (or overnight for best results)."
  - "Heat oil in a heavy-bottomed pan. Cook marinated chicken pieces until lightly charred on all sides. Set aside."
  - "In the same pan, melt 2 tbsp butter. Saut\u00e9 onions until golden brown, about 8-10 minutes."
  - "Add garlic and ginger, cook for 1 minute until fragrant."
  - "Pour in the tomato puree. Cook on medium heat for 15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce thickens and oil separates."
  - "Add the cooked chicken to the sauce. Simmer for 10 minutes."
  - "Stir in heavy cream, sugar, and remaining 1 tbsp butter. Cook for 5 more minutes."
  - "Crush kasuri methi between your palms and stir into the curry."
  - "Garnish with fresh cilantro. Serve hot with naan or basmati rice."
notes: "For a smoky flavor, grill the marinated chicken on a BBQ or under a broiler before adding to the sauce. The sauce can be made a day ahead and tastes even better the next day."
