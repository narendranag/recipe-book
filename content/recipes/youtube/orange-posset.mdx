servings: '2'
ingredients:
- 1/4 cup fresh orange juice (about 1 orange or tangerine)
- 250ml fresh milk cream
- 1/4 cup granulated sugar (50 grams)
- Pinch of salt
- Orange zest for garnish
instructions:
- Extract juice from 1 orange or tangerine and measure out 1/4 cup of juice.
- Heat the fresh milk cream with the granulated sugar and a pinch of salt until warm
  and sugar dissolves.
- Stir in the orange juice until the mixture thickens slightly.
- Pour into serving cups (you may use the hollowed orange halves as cups if desired).
- Chill until set and serve garnished with fresh orange zest.
notes: Orange halves can be used as natural serving cups for an elegant presentation.
  The posset requires chilling time before serving.
