ingredients:
- 2 cups full cream milk
- 250g thickened cream (whipping cream)
- 1-1½ teaspoons kewra water (optional)
- ½ cup Greek yogurt
- 2 tablespoons poppy seeds
instructions:
- Combine the full cream milk and thickened cream in a heavy-bottomed pot.
- Stir in the Greek yogurt until well incorporated.
- Add the poppy seeds and mix thoroughly.
- Add kewra water if using, stirring gently to distribute the flavor.
- Simmer over medium heat, stirring occasionally, until the sauce reaches your desired
  consistency and flavors meld together.
