ingredients:
- 500 grams    Basmati rice
- 1 tsp black cumin
- 2 number    boiled egg for decoration (optional)
- 50 grams    Cashew nuts (Optional)
- 1 bunch    coriander leaves
- 2 cup Curd
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 2 tbsp Garam masala powder
- 2 tbsp    Garlic and ginger paste
- 2 cup Golden fried sliced onions
- 3 number gr chilli
- 2 number    Lime juice
- 1 tsp meat tenderizer
- 1 bunch mint
- 1000 grams    Mutton
- 5 tbsp Oil
- 1 tbsp Red chilli pdr
- 1 tbsp Rose Water (optional)
- 1 pinch    Saffron
- 1 to taste    Salt
- 1 pinch    Turmeric powder
instructions:
- First boil the water ,add oil, salt and cook rice half cooked.
- Next maranation of mutton, in pan take mutton, add all the spices and curd, onion,
- Now n a pan add the maraniated mutton in the bottom and top it of with half cooked
  rice,add mint leaves, coriander leaves, fried onions.repeat the same process twice.cook
  for 30mins
- serve hot with raita and mirchi ka salan , follow the video recipoe of bagara baingan
  , make a thinner gravy ,substitute brinjal,eggplant with green chillies
