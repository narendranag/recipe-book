instructions:
- Slice boneless chicken thinly for even cooking.
- In a mixing bowl, combine mustard oil and Kashmiri red chilli powder and mix well
  to release the color.
- Add ginger-garlic paste, coriander powder, cumin powder, turmeric powder, kasuri
  methi, amchur powder, black salt, garam masala, fresh mint, fresh coriander, and
  salt to the oil mixture.
- Add sliced chicken to the marinade, coat well, and refrigerate for at least 1-2
  hours, or up to 20 hours for maximum flavor.
- 'Smoke the marinated chicken for 4-5 minutes to impart smoky flavor (optional: can
  be done at marination stage, after grilling, or before serving).'
- Heat a grill pan and grill the marinated chicken on both sides until golden brown,
  or bake in an oven at 220°C for 5-6 minutes.
- Cool the grilled chicken and shred into thin strips. Set aside.
- 'To make the makhani gravy base: Heat oil in a stock pot on medium heat, add cumin
  seeds and sliced onions, and sauté until light golden brown.'
- Add garlic, ginger, green chillies, powdered spices (red chilli powder, coriander
  powder, turmeric powder, kasuri methi), whole red chillies, and green cardamom.
  Cook for 1 minute.
- Add tomatoes, cashew nuts, coriander stems with roots, salt, and butter. Mix well
  and cook on medium-high heat for 5 minutes.
- Lower heat, cover, and cook for 25-30 minutes on medium-low heat, stirring occasionally.
  Add hot water as needed if the mixture dries out.
- Once tomatoes are fully cooked, switch off heat and let the mixture cool to room
  temperature.
- Transfer the cooled mixture to a blender and grind into a fine puree. Strain through
  a sieve and set aside.
- 'For the final butter chicken: Heat butter and oil in a wok on medium heat, add
  chopped onions, ginger, garlic, and green chillies. Sauté until onions are translucent.'
- Lower heat and add Kashmiri red chilli powder, cook for 30 seconds to 1 minute.
- Add the strained makhani gravy base and adjust consistency with hot water as needed.
- Add sugar or honey and cook for 15-20 minutes on medium flame, stirring occasionally.
- Add the shredded grilled chicken and stir well. Add hot water to adjust consistency
  if needed.
- Cook for 5-6 minutes on medium flame.
- Add toasted kasuri methi and garam masala, mix well, and adjust seasoning with salt
  as needed.
- Lower heat, add butter and fresh cream, and stir well. Cook for 1 minute without
  overcooking.
- Finish with freshly chopped coriander leaves.
- Serve hot with butter garlic paratha, naan, or Indian bread of choice.
notes: 'Marinate chicken for at least 1-2 hours, but can be refrigerated up to 20
  hours for deeper flavor. Smoking is optional and can be done at three stages: during
  marination for subtle flavor, after grilling, or before serving for dominant smoke
  flavor. Do not add too much water while cooking tomatoes; let them release their
  natural moisture. Cool the gravy base to room temperature before grinding for smoother
  puree. Do not overcook after adding cream to maintain its richness.'
