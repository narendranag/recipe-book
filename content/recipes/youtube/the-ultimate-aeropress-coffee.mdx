ingredients:
- 11g coffee, ground at the finer end of medium
- 200g water, brought to a boil
instructions:
- Insert the filter into the cap without rinsing or preheating the brewer.
- Place the brewer on a scale and add the ground coffee.
- Pour the hot water over the coffee, aiming to wet all the grounds during pouring.
- Start a timer and immediately insert the piston piece into the top of the brewer
  about 1 cm deep.
- Wait 2 minutes.
- Holding the brewer and piston together, gently swirl the brewer.
- Wait 30 seconds.
- Press the piston down gently all the way through.
- Serve and enjoy.
notes: This technique is optimized for light roasted coffee. For darker roasts, you
  may prefer to increase the dose and coarsen the grind. Water should be used immediately
  after boiling.
