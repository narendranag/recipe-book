ingredients:
- 2-3 onions, cubed
- 2 tbsp ginger, peeled and chopped
- 6-8 garlic cloves, peeled
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 2-3 green chillies, chopped
- Curry leaves, washed
- Oil for cooking
- 1 tsp mustard seeds
- 3 tbsp tomato purée
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp red chilli powder
- Salt to taste
- 3 tbsp water
- 1 cup coconut milk
- 200 ml water
- 800 g boneless fish fillets, cut into thick slices
- 1 tbsp white vinegar
instructions:
- Make a paste with the cubed onions, peeled and chopped ginger, peeled garlic, and
  cumin seeds. Set aside.
- Chop the green chillies and wash the curry leaves.
- Prepare the coconut milk (use readymade or make fresh from coconut at home).
- Cut the boneless fish fillets into thick slices.
- Heat oil in a pan and add the mustard seeds. Once they splutter, add the curry leaves
  and chopped green chillies.
- Stir and add the onion paste. Mix well and fry on medium heat for 7-8 minutes until
  light brown.
- Add the tomato purée, mix well, and fry on low heat for 2 minutes.
- Add the turmeric, red chilli powder, and salt. Mix well and fry on medium heat for
  2-3 minutes after adding 3 tbsp water, until oil separates.
- Add the coconut milk and 200 ml water. Mix well, cover, and cook on low heat for
  5 minutes until oil separates.
- Add the boneless fish pieces, mix gently, cover, and cook on low heat for 10 minutes.
- Add the white vinegar and shake the pan to mix. Simmer on low heat for 2 minutes.
- Switch off heat, cover, and let rest for 15-30 minutes before serving.
- Serve with steamed rice.
notes: You can use readymade coconut milk or make fresh coconut milk at home. Resting
  the curry for 15-30 minutes after cooking allows the flavors to meld together beautifully.
  Serve with steamed rice or flatbread.
