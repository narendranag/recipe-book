ingredients:
- For Frying
- ⅓ cup Mustard oil, सरसों का तेल
- 2 large Potatoes (peeled & diced) आलू
- 8-9 no. hard boiled Eggs, उबले हुए अंडे
- For Dimer Kosha
- ⅓ cup Remaining Mustard oil, सरसों का तेल
- 1 no. Bay leaf, तेज़ पत्ता
- 3 no. dry Red chillies, सूखी लाल मिर्च
- 1 tsp Cumin seeds, जीरा
- 2 ½ large Onions, sliced, प्याज
- Prepared masala mixture, तैयार किया हुआ मसाला मिश्रण
- 3 medium Tomatoes, chopped, टमाटर
- Fried potatoes, तले हुए आलू
- ¼ cup Water, पानी
- 2 no. Green chillies (less spicy & roughly torn) हरी मिर्च
- ½ cup Water, पानी
- Salt to taste, नमक स्वादअनुसार
- Fried Eggs, तले हुए अंडे
- For Masala Mixture
- 1 tbsp Coriander powder, धनिया पाउडर
- ¼ tsp Cumin powder, जीरा पाउडर
- 1 heaped tsp Degi red chili powder, देगी लाल मिर्च पाउडर
- ½ tsp Turmeric powder, हल्दी पाउडर
- 1 tbsp Ginger Garlic paste, अदरक लहसुन का पेस्ट
- ¼ cup Water, पानी
- For Garnish
- Coriander sprig, धनिया पत्ता
- Process
- For Frying
- ● In a non-stick kadai, add mustard oil, once it's smoky hot, add potatoes and fry
  them until golden brown or until cooked well.
- ● Transfer it to an absorbent paper and keep it aside.
- ● Add boiled eggs and fry them for a while until golden brown.
- ● Transfer it to an absorbent paper and keep it aside for further use.
- For Dimer Kosha
- ● In the same kadai, add mustard oil, once it's smoky hot, add bay leaf, dry red
  chillies, cumin seed and let it splutter well.
- ● Add onions and saute it for a while until golden in color.
- ● Add prepared masala mixture and cook it for 2-3 minutes.
- ● Add tomatoes, fried potatoes, water, green chillies, cover it with the lid and
  cook for a while until the tomatoes are mushy.
- ● Add water to adjust the consistency, salt to taste, fried eggs and give it a quick
  boil.
- ● Transfer it to a serving dish and garnish it with coriander sprig.
- ● Serve hot with paratha or steam rice.
- For Masala Mixture
- ● In a bowl, add coriander powder, cumin powder, degi red chili powder, turmeric
  powder, ginger garlic paste, water and give it a good mix.
- ● Keep it aside for further use.
