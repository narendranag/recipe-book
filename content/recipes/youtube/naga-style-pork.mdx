servings: '4'
ingredients:
- Fresh pork, cut into pieces
- Fermented bamboo shoots with water
instructions:
- Clean the pork pieces and cut into desired size.
- In a pot or cooking vessel, combine the pork with the fermented bamboo shoots and
  their water.
- Cook over medium heat until the pork is tender and fully cooked through, approximately
  30-40 minutes.
- Adjust seasoning to taste and serve hot.
notes: This is a traditional Naga recipe from Dimapur, Nagaland. The fermented bamboo
  shoots are essential to the dish's authentic flavor. Adjust cooking time based on
  the size of your pork pieces.
