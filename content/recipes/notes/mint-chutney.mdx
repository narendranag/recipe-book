description: Mint Chutney
ingredients:
- 1 cup fresh mint leaves (packed)
- ½ cup fresh cilantro (coriander) leaves (packed)
- 1 small green chili (adjust to taste)
- 1-inch piece of ginger
- 1 garlic clove (optional)
- ½ teaspoon cumin seeds or ground cumin
- ½ teaspoon chaat masala (optional, for extra tang)
- ½ teaspoon salt (adjust to taste)
- 1 teaspoon sugar (balances the flavors)
- 2 tablespoons lemon juice (or to taste)
- 2–3 tablespoons water (as needed for blending)
- 2 tablespoons yogurt (optional, for creaminess)
instructions:
- 'Prepare ingredients: Rinse the mint and cilantro leaves well, discarding thick
  stems.'
- 'Blend: In a blender or food processor, add mint, cilantro, green chili, ginger,
  garlic (if using), cumin, chaat masala, salt, sugar, and lemon juice.'
- 'Adjust consistency: Add water gradually while blending until you get a smooth paste.'
- 'Optional creaminess: If using yogurt, mix it in after blending for a smoother,
  milder chutney.'
- 'Taste and adjust: Add more salt, sugar, or lemon juice as needed.'
- 'Store: Keep refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 3 days. You can also
  freeze it in ice cube trays for longer storage.'
