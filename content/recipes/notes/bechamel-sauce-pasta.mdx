description: Bechamel Sauce Pasta White Sauce Pasta Recipe (Creamy Bechamel Pasta)
ingredients:
- For the White Sauce (Béchamel)
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1.5 cups milk (warm)
- ½ tsp black pepper (freshly ground)
- ½ tsp salt (adjust to taste)
- ¼ tsp nutmeg (optional, for flavor)
- ¼ cup grated cheese (cheddar, parmesan, or mozzarella)
- For the Pasta
- 2 cups pasta (penne, fusilli, or macaroni)
- 4 cups water
- ½ tsp salt
- 1 tsp olive oil
- For Additional Flavor & Veggies (Optional)
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter
- ½ cup onions (finely chopped)
- ½ cup bell peppers (diced)
- ½ cup mushrooms (sliced)
- ½ cup boiled sweet corn
- 2 cloves garlic (minced)
instructions:
- 'Step 1: Cook the Pasta'
- Boil 4 cups of water in a large pot. Add salt and a teaspoon of olive oil.
- Add the pasta and cook according to the package instructions (usually 8-10 minutes)
  until al dente.
- Drain and set aside.
- 'Step 2: Make the White Sauce'
- Heat butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the flour and whisk continuously for
  1-2 minutes until it turns light golden (don’t brown it too much).
- Gradually pour in warm milk, stirring constantly to avoid lumps.
- Cook until the sauce thickens (around 3-4 minutes). Add salt, black pepper, and
  nutmeg (if using).
- Stir in the grated cheese and mix until smooth. Remove from heat.
- 'Step 3: Cook the Veggies (Optional)'
- In another pan, heat olive oil or butter. Add garlic and sauté for 30 seconds.
- Add onions, bell peppers, mushrooms, and sweet corn. Sauté for 3-4 minutes until
  slightly tender.
- 'Step 4: Combine Everything'
- Add the cooked pasta and sautéed veggies into the white sauce. Mix well to coat
  everything evenly.
- Cook for another 2 minutes on low heat to let the flavors blend.
- 'Step 5: Serve'
- Garnish with more grated cheese and fresh parsley. Serve hot!
- Enjoy your creamy white sauce pasta!
