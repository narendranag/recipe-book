description: Chicken Biryani Masala Here’s an easy recipe to make Biryani Masala at
  home. Freshly made masala adds an incredible depth of flavor to your biryani! Ingredients
  (Makes about 1/2 cup)
ingredients:
- 2 tablespoons cumin seeds
- 2 tablespoons coriander seeds
- 2 teaspoons fennel seeds
- 1 tablespoon black peppercorns
- 1 tablespoon cloves
- 2-3 large bay leaves
- 4-5 green cardamom pods
- 2-3 black cardamom pods
- 2 teaspoons caraway seeds (shahi jeera, optional)
- 2-inch cinnamon stick
- 2-3 star anise
- 1 small piece of mace (or 1/4 teaspoon ground mace)
- 1 teaspoon nutmeg powder (optional)
- 'Ground Spices:'
- 2 teaspoons turmeric powder
- 2 teaspoons red chili powder (adjust to taste)
- 1 teaspoon dried ginger powder (or fresh ginger, optional)
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon (optional for added aroma)
instructions:
- Dry Roast the Whole Spices
- Heat a pan on low-medium heat.
- Add all the whole spices (except nutmeg and mace) and roast for 2-3 minutes until
  aromatic. Stir frequently to prevent burning.
- Remove from heat and let the spices cool completely.
- Grind the Spices
- Transfer the roasted whole spices, mace, and nutmeg to a spice grinder or a blender.
- Grind to a fine powder.
- Mix in Ground Spices
- Add turmeric powder, chili powder, and dried ginger powder to the ground mixture.
- Mix well to combine.
- Store
- Store the biryani masala in an airtight container in a cool, dry place. It stays
  fresh for up to 2 months. For longer shelf life, refrigerate it.
- Usage
- Use 1-2 teaspoons of this masala for a single biryani recipe (serving 4-6 people).
  Adjust to taste.
- You can also use this masala for curries, pulao, or grilled chicken/mutton dishes.
- Enjoy the rich and authentic flavors this masala brings to your biryani!
