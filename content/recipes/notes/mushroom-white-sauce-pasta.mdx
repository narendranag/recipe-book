description: 'Mushroom White Sauce Pasta Here’s a simple and delicious recipe for
  mushroom white sauce pasta:'
ingredients:
- 'Pasta: 250g (any type, like fettuccine, penne, or spaghetti)'
- 'Mushrooms: 200g, sliced'
- 'Butter: 3 tbsp'
- 'Garlic: 3 cloves, minced'
- 'All-purpose flour: 2 tbsp'
- 'Milk: 2 cups (warm)'
- 'Heavy cream (optional): 1/4 cup'
- 'Parmesan cheese: 1/2 cup, grated'
- 'Salt: to taste'
- 'Black pepper: to taste'
- 'Nutmeg: a pinch (optional)'
- 'Fresh parsley: for garnish'
- 'Olive oil: 1 tbsp'
instructions:
- 'Cook the pasta:'
- Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil and cook the pasta according to the
  package instructions. Drain and set aside, reserving a cup of the pasta water.
- 'Sauté the mushrooms:'
- In a large skillet, heat the olive oil and 1 tbsp of butter over medium-high heat.
- Add the sliced mushrooms and sauté until golden brown and any moisture has evaporated
  (about 5-6 minutes). Set the mushrooms aside.
- 'Prepare the white sauce:'
- In the same skillet, reduce the heat to medium and melt the remaining 2 tbsp of
  butter.
- Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 30 seconds, or until fragrant.
- Stir in the flour and cook for 1-2 minutes, stirring continuously to remove the
  raw taste of the flour.
- Gradually pour in the warm milk, whisking continuously to avoid lumps. Allow it
  to simmer and thicken for 3-4 minutes.
- Stir in the heavy cream (if using), grated Parmesan cheese, and season with salt,
  black pepper, and a pinch of nutmeg (optional). Simmer until the sauce is smooth
  and creamy.
- 'Combine the pasta and mushrooms:'
- Add the sautéed mushrooms to the sauce and stir.
- Toss in the cooked pasta, mixing until it’s fully coated in the white sauce. Add
  a bit of the reserved pasta water if the sauce is too thick.
- 'Serve:'
- Garnish with fresh parsley and extra Parmesan cheese, if desired.
- Serve warm and enjoy!
