title: Chicken Rice
slug: chicken-rice
description: A fragrant Asian-inspired chicken and rice dish for two, infused with
  ginger, garlic, and a savory sesame-soy sauce, topped with fresh spring onions and
  crispy chilli.
source: notes
rewritten: true
category:
- Main Course
cuisine: Chinese
prepTime: 15 min
cookTime: 30 min
totalTime: 45 min
servings: '2'
ingredients:
- 1 cup rice, washed
- 1 cup chicken stock
- Chicken breast or thighs
- 1 tablespoon sesame oil
- 1 tablespoon ginger, minced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 tablespoons soya sauce
- 1 tablespoon oyster sauce
- 1 tablespoon mirin
- 1 teaspoon sesame oil (for sauce)
- 1-2 green chillies, sliced
- Spring onion whites, chopped
- Spring onion greens, sliced (for garnish)
- Crunchy chilli sauce, for serving
instructions:
- Rub the chicken with sesame oil and season with salt and pepper.
- Cook the chicken in a pan until golden and cooked through. Set aside and slice or
  cut into pieces.
- In the same pan, sauté the spring onion whites, ginger, and garlic until fragrant.
- Add the washed rice and stir to coat with oil.
- Pour in the chicken stock and bring to a boil. Reduce heat, cover, and simmer until
  rice is cooked and liquid is absorbed, about 15-20 minutes.
- While rice cooks, prepare the sauce by mixing soya sauce, oyster sauce, mirin, sesame
  oil, and sliced green chillies.
- Once rice is cooked, fluff with a fork and stir in the sliced chicken and sauce
  until well combined.
- Garnish with spring onion greens and serve with crunchy chilli sauce on the side.
notes: Rub sesame oil on the chicken before cooking to enhance flavor. The sauce can
  be adjusted to taste by adding more soya sauce for saltiness or more mirin for sweetness.
