description: Chicken Biryani Here’s a delicious Chicken Biryani recipe you can make
  at home. This is a simplified yet flavorful version of the classic dish. Ingredients
  (Serves 4-6)
ingredients:
- 500g chicken (bone-in or boneless, cut into pieces)
- 1 cup yogurt
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1 teaspoon cumin powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- Salt to taste
- Juice of half a lemon
- 'For the Rice:'
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 4 cups water
- 3-4 green cardamom pods
- 4-5 cloves
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 'For the Biryani Base:'
- 2 large onions, thinly sliced
- 2 large tomatoes, chopped
- 3-4 green chilies, slit
- 1/4 cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, chopped
- 1/2 cup cooking oil or ghee
- 1/2 teaspoon saffron strands soaked in 2 tablespoons warm milk (optional)
- 1 teaspoon biryani masala (store-bought or homemade)
- 2 tablespoons fried onions (optional, for garnish)
instructions:
- 'Step 1: Marinate the Chicken'
- In a large bowl, mix all the marinade ingredients.
- Add the chicken pieces, coat well, and let it marinate for at least 1 hour (preferably
  overnight in the fridge).
- 'Step 2: Prepare the Rice'
- Wash the basmati rice thoroughly and soak it in water for 30 minutes. Drain before
  cooking.
- In a large pot, bring 4 cups of water to a boil. Add cardamom, cloves, cinnamon,
  bay leaf, and salt.
- Add the drained rice and cook until it’s about 70% cooked (it should still have
  a bite to it). Drain and set aside.
- 'Step 3: Cook the Chicken'
- Heat oil or ghee in a large heavy-bottomed pan.
- Add the sliced onions and sauté until golden brown (reserve some for garnish if
  desired).
- Add the slit green chilies, followed by the chopped tomatoes. Cook until the tomatoes
  turn soft and the oil separates.
- Add the marinated chicken and cook until the chicken is mostly cooked and the masala
  thickens (about 15 minutes). Add water if necessary to prevent sticking.
- 'Step 4: Assemble the Biryani'
- Lower the heat. Spread half the cooked rice over the chicken mixture in the pan.
- Sprinkle half the mint, coriander leaves, and saffron milk over the rice.
- Add the remaining rice and repeat the layering with the herbs and saffron.
- Cover the pot tightly with a lid (use aluminum foil to seal it if necessary) and
  cook on very low heat (dum) for 20-25 minutes.
- 'Step 5: Serve'
- Gently mix the layers just before serving to combine the flavors.
- Garnish with fried onions and serve hot with raita, salad, or boiled eggs.
