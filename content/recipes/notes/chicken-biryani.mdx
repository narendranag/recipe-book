title: Chicken Biryani
slug: chicken-biryani
description: A fragrant and flavorful one-pot rice dish with tender marinated chicken,
  aromatic spices, and layers of herbs. This simplified yet authentic version of the
  classic biryani is perfect for home cooking.
source: notes
rewritten: true
category:
- Main Course
cuisine: Indian
prepTime: 1 hour
cookTime: 1 hour
totalTime: 2 hours 25 minutes
servings: 4-6
ingredients:
- 500g chicken, bone-in or boneless, cut into pieces
- 1 cup yogurt
- 1 tablespoon ginger-garlic paste
- 1 teaspoon turmeric powder
- 1 teaspoon red chili powder
- 1 teaspoon garam masala
- 1 teaspoon cumin powder
- 1 teaspoon coriander powder
- Salt to taste
- Juice of half a lemon
- 2 cups basmati rice
- 4 cups water
- 3-4 green cardamom pods
- 4-5 cloves
- 1 small cinnamon stick
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 large onions, thinly sliced
- 2 large tomatoes, chopped
- 3-4 green chilies, slit
- 1/4 cup fresh coriander leaves, chopped
- 1/4 cup fresh mint leaves, chopped
- 1/2 cup cooking oil or ghee
- 1/2 teaspoon saffron strands soaked in 2 tablespoons warm milk (optional)
- 1 teaspoon biryani masala
- 2 tablespoons fried onions, for garnish (optional)
instructions:
- In a large bowl, mix yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, turmeric powder, red chili powder,
  garam masala, cumin powder, coriander powder, salt, and lemon juice.
- Add chicken pieces, coat well, and marinate for at least 1 hour, preferably overnight
  in the fridge.
- Wash basmati rice thoroughly and soak in water for 30 minutes. Drain before cooking.
- In a large pot, bring 4 cups of water to a boil. Add green cardamom pods, cloves,
  cinnamon stick, and bay leaf with salt.
- Add the drained rice and cook until about 70% cooked (it should still have a bite
  to it). Drain and set aside.
- Heat oil or ghee in a large heavy-bottomed pan over medium heat.
- Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown, reserving some for garnish.
- Add slit green chilies, then chopped tomatoes. Cook until tomatoes are soft and
  oil separates from the masala.
- Add marinated chicken and cook until mostly cooked and the masala thickens, about
  15 minutes. Add water if needed to prevent sticking.
- Lower heat. Spread half the cooked rice over the chicken mixture in the pan.
- Sprinkle half the mint and coriander leaves over the rice, then drizzle with half
  the saffron milk.
- Add the remaining rice and repeat the layering with herbs and saffron.
- Cover the pot tightly with a lid, sealing with aluminum foil if necessary. Cook
  on very low heat (dum) for 20-25 minutes.
- Gently mix the layers just before serving. Garnish with fried onions and serve hot
  with raita, salad, or boiled eggs.
notes: Marinating the chicken overnight yields better flavor. Use a heavy-bottomed
  pot with a tight-fitting lid for proper dum cooking. The rice should be parboiled
  to 70% doneness so it finishes cooking with the chicken and absorbs all the flavors.
  Saffron and fried onions are optional but add authentic richness to the dish.
