title: Pasta with Ground Beef
slug: pasta-with-ground-beef
description: A hearty and easy weeknight pasta dish featuring seasoned ground beef
  in a rich tomato sauce, tossed with your favorite pasta and finished with Parmesan
  cheese.
source: notes
rewritten: true
category:

cuisine: Italian
prepTime: 15 min
cookTime: 25 min
totalTime: 40 min
servings: 2-3
ingredients:
- 200g pasta (penne, spaghetti, or your favorite kind)
- 200g ground beef
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup tomato paste or 1 cup canned crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup beef broth or water
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/4 tsp chili flakes (optional, for heat)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese, plus extra for serving
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley or basil, chopped, for garnish
instructions:
- Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the pasta according to package instructions
  until al dente. Drain and set aside, reserving 1/2 cup of pasta water.
- Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the ground beef and cook
  until browned, breaking it up into small pieces with a spoon. Season with salt and
  pepper. Remove and set aside.
- In the same skillet, add the onion and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened.
- Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
- Stir in the tomato paste or crushed tomatoes and beef broth. Mix well.
- Add oregano, basil, chili flakes if using, and adjust seasoning with salt and pepper.
  Simmer for 5-7 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
- Return the cooked ground beef to the skillet and mix well.
- Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss to coat. If the sauce is too thick,
  add a splash of the reserved pasta water.
- Stir in Parmesan cheese until melted and combined.
- Garnish with parsley or basil and more grated Parmesan. Serve hot, optionally with
  garlic bread or a simple side salad.
notes: Reserve pasta water to adjust sauce consistency. To halve the recipe for one
  serving, reduce all ingredient quantities by half. For extra heat, increase chili
  flakes to taste.
