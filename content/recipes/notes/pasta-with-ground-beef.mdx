description: Pasta with Ground Beef Here’s a hearty Pasta with Ground Beef recipe
  that’s easy and delicious! Ground Beef Pasta
ingredients:
- 200g pasta (penne, spaghetti, or your favorite kind)
- 200g ground beef
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 1 small onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup tomato paste or 1 cup canned crushed tomatoes
- 1/2 cup beef broth (or water)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp dried basil
- 1/4 tsp chili flakes (optional, for heat)
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese (plus extra for serving)
- 1/4 cup fresh parsley or basil (chopped, for garnish)
instructions:
- Cook the Pasta
- Boil a large pot of salted water and cook the pasta according to package instructions
  until al dente. Drain and set aside, reserving 1/2 cup of pasta water.
- Cook the Ground Beef
- Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat.
- Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up into small pieces with
  a spoon. Season with salt and pepper. Remove and set aside.
- Sauté the Aromatics
- In the same skillet, add the onion and cook for 3-4 minutes until softened.
- Add garlic and cook for 30 seconds until fragrant.
- Make the Sauce
- Stir in the tomato paste (or crushed tomatoes) and beef broth. Mix well.
- Add oregano, basil, chili flakes (if using), and adjust seasoning with salt and
  pepper.
- Simmer for 5-7 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly.
- Combine Everything
- Return the cooked ground beef to the skillet and mix well.
- Add the cooked pasta to the skillet and toss to coat. If the sauce is too thick,
  add a splash of the reserved pasta water.
- Finish with Cheese and Herbs
- Stir in Parmesan cheese until melted and combined.
- Garnish with parsley or basil and more grated Parmesan.
- Serve
- Plate the pasta and serve hot. Optionally, pair with garlic bread or a simple side
  salad.
- This recipe serves 2-3 people. To adjust for one, halve the ingredients. 😊
