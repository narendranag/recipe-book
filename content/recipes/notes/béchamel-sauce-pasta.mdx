title: Béchamel Sauce Pasta

description: A creamy and comforting pasta dish coated in a rich béchamel white sauce
  with optional vegetables. This classic Italian-inspired recipe is simple to make
  and perfect for a weeknight dinner.

prepTime: 10 min
cookTime: 20 min
totalTime: 30 min
servings: 2-3
ingredients:
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1.5 cups milk, warm
- ½ tsp salt, adjust to taste
- ½ tsp black pepper, freshly ground
- ¼ tsp nutmeg, optional
- ¼ cup grated cheese (cheddar, parmesan, or mozzarella)
- 2 cups pasta (penne, fusilli, or macaroni)
- 4 cups water
- 1 tsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp olive oil or butter, for sautéing
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- ½ cup onions, finely chopped
- ½ cup bell peppers, diced
- ½ cup mushrooms, sliced
- ½ cup boiled sweet corn
instructions:
- Boil 4 cups of water in a large pot. Add salt and olive oil.
- Add the pasta and cook according to package instructions (usually 8-10 minutes)
  until al dente. Drain and set aside.
- Heat butter in a pan over medium heat. Add the flour and whisk continuously for
  1-2 minutes until light golden.
- Gradually pour in warm milk, stirring constantly to avoid lumps.
- Cook until the sauce thickens (around 3-4 minutes). Add salt, black pepper, and
  nutmeg if using.
- Stir in the grated cheese and mix until smooth. Remove from heat.
- In another pan, heat olive oil or butter. Add garlic and sauté for 30 seconds.
- Add onions, bell peppers, mushrooms, and sweet corn. Sauté for 3-4 minutes until
  slightly tender.
- Add the cooked pasta and sautéed vegetables into the white sauce. Mix well to coat
  everything evenly.
- Cook for another 2 minutes on low heat to let the flavors blend.
- Garnish with grated cheese and fresh parsley. Serve hot.
notes: The vegetables are optional—you can skip them if preferred. Do not brown the
  flour roux too much; a light golden color ensures a smooth sauce. The sauce will
  continue to thicken slightly as it cools.
