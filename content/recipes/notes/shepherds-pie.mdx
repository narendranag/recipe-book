description: Shepherd’s Pie Here’s a classic Shepherd’s Pie recipe with a rich, flavorful
  meat filling and creamy mashed potatoes on top.
ingredients:
- 'For the meat filling:'
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 2 carrots, finely diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 500g (1 lb) ground lamb (or beef for Cottage Pie)
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 cup beef broth
- 1/2 cup red wine (optional, for depth of flavor)
- 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
- 1 tsp fresh thyme (or ½ tsp dried)
- 1 tsp fresh rosemary (or ½ tsp dried)
- 1/2 cup frozen peas
- Salt and pepper to taste
- 'For the mashed potato topping:'
- 1 kg (2 lbs) potatoes, peeled and cut into chunks
- 4 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup milk or heavy cream
- 1/2 cup shredded cheddar cheese (optional, for extra richness)
- Salt and pepper to taste
instructions:
- 'Cook the meat filling:'
- Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat.
- Sauté onions, carrots, and garlic until softened, about 5 minutes.
- Add the ground lamb (or beef) and cook until browned, breaking it up as it cooks.
  Drain excess fat if needed.
- Stir in tomato paste and cook for 1-2 minutes.
- Pour in red wine (if using) and let it simmer until reduced by half.
- Add beef broth, Worcestershire sauce, thyme, and rosemary. Simmer for 10-15 minutes
  until thickened.
- Stir in frozen peas, season with salt and pepper, then remove from heat.
- 'Make the mashed potatoes:'
- Boil the potatoes in salted water until fork-tender, about 15 minutes.
- Drain and mash with butter, milk (or cream), and cheddar cheese (if using).
- Season with salt and pepper to taste.
- 'Assemble and bake:'
- Preheat oven to 400°F (200°C).
- Spread the meat filling evenly in a baking dish.
- Spoon mashed potatoes over the top and smooth with a spatula. Use a fork to create
  ridges for a crispy texture.
- Bake for 20-25 minutes until golden brown on top.
- Let it rest for 5 minutes before serving.
