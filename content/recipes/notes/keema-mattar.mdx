ingredients:
- Crushed garlic
- Black pepper
- Coriander
- Cardamom
- Ghee
instructions:
- Heat ghee in a pan over medium heat.
- Add crushed garlic and cook for 2-4 minutes until fragrant.
- Add ground meat and cook for 5 minutes, breaking it apart as it cooks.
- Stir in coriander, cardamom, and black pepper.
- Add peas and cook until the meat is cooked through and peas are tender.
