title: Bhuna Keema
slug: bhuna-keema
description: A rich and aromatic mutton keema dish where ground mutton is slow-cooked
  with yogurt and spices, then tempered with ghee and finished with fresh herbs. This
  dry, concentrated curry is bursting with complex flavors from roasted spices.
source: notes
rewritten: true
category:
- Main Course
cuisine: Indian
prepTime: 10 min
cookTime: 45 min
totalTime: 55 min
servings: 4-6
ingredients:
- 1 kg mutton keema (ground mutton)
- 1 tbsp red chilli powder
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1½ cups yogurt, beaten
- Salt to taste
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 2-3 green chillies, slit
- 1 whole nutmeg
- 5-6 mutton bones
- 3 tbsp ghee
- ¼ cup ghee (for tempering)
- 2 tbsp oil
- 1 bay leaf
- 2 cloves
- 10-12 black peppercorns
- 2 black cardamom
- 3-4 onions, chopped
- 1 cup yogurt, beaten
- 1 tbsp Degi red chilli powder
- 2 tbsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- A pinch of asafoetida
- ½ cup green peas
- 3-4 green cardamom
- 3-4 cloves (for masala)
- 3 tbsp coriander stems, chopped
- 1-2 green chillies, chopped
- Handful of coriander leaves, chopped
- Handful of mint leaves, chopped
instructions:
- 'Prepare the masala: In a grinder, combine black peppercorns, cloves, black cardamom,
  and green cardamom. Grind into a powder and set aside.'
- In a bowl, mix mutton keema with red chilli powder, turmeric powder, 1½ cups beaten
  yogurt, salt, ginger-garlic paste, green chillies, whole nutmeg, and mutton bones.
  Mix well.
- Transfer the mixture to a pressure cooker, add 3 tbsp ghee, cover, and cook for
  2-3 whistles until the meat is tender.
- While the meat cooks, heat ¼ cup ghee and 2 tbsp oil in a large saucepan over medium-high
  heat.
- Add bay leaf, cloves, black peppercorns, and black cardamom to the hot ghee and
  oil. Let the spices splutter for 30 seconds.
- Add chopped onions and sauté until light golden brown.
- In a bowl, combine 1 cup beaten yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, Degi red chilli powder,
  coriander powder, turmeric powder, cumin powder, and asafoetida. Mix well.
- Pour this yogurt mixture into the saucepan with the onions and sauté well.
- Remove the mutton bones and whole nutmeg from the cooked keema. Add the keema to
  the saucepan and mix well.
- Stir in the coriander stems, green peas, and prepared masala powder. Mix thoroughly.
- Cover and cook for 2 minutes.
- Add chopped green chillies, coriander leaves, and mint leaves. Mix gently.
- Garnish with additional coriander leaves and serve hot with roti or pav.
notes: The whole nutmeg and mutton bones are removed after the initial pressure cooking
  but add depth to the flavor. Bhuna keema should have a dry, concentrated consistency
  with the meat well-coated in the spice mixture. Serve immediately after garnishing
  for best flavor.
