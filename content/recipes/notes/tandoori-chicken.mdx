description: 'Tandoori Chicken Making tandoori chicken in the oven is straightforward
  and delivers great results. Here’s a simple recipe for you:'
ingredients:
- 4 chicken drumsticks or thighs (with or without skin)
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
- 1 tbsp red chili powder (adjust to taste)
- 1 tsp turmeric powder
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 1 tsp cumin powder
- 1 tsp coriander powder
- 1 tsp smoked paprika (optional, for color)
- 1 tbsp mustard oil (or any cooking oil)
- Salt, to taste
- Red/orange food color (optional, for authentic tandoori look)
instructions:
- Marinate the Chicken
- Make deep slits on the chicken pieces for better marination.
- In a bowl, mix yogurt, lemon juice, ginger-garlic paste, spices, salt, oil, and
  food coloring (if using).
- Coat the chicken thoroughly with the marinade, making sure it gets into the slits.
- Cover and let it marinate in the refrigerator for at least 4 hours or overnight
  for best results.
- Preheat the Oven
- Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
- Prepare for Baking
- Line a baking tray with aluminum foil and place a wire rack on top. This allows
  the chicken to cook evenly and avoids sogginess.
- Arrange the marinated chicken on the rack, shaking off excess marinade.
- Bake
- Bake the chicken in the preheated oven for 25–30 minutes.
- Halfway through (around 15 minutes), flip the chicken pieces and brush them with
  a little oil or leftover marinade for extra flavor.
- Broil (Optional for Char Marks)
- After baking, set your oven to broil and cook for 3–5 minutes to get a slight charred
  effect, similar to a tandoor.
- Serve
- Serve hot with mint chutney, onion slices, and lemon wedges.
- Enjoy your oven-roasted tandoori chicken!
