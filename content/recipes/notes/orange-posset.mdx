description: Orange Posset (Serves 6)
ingredients:
- 3 cups (750ml) heavy cream
- 1 cup (195g) granulated sugar
- 1/2 cup (120ml) fresh orange juice (about 3 oranges)
- 1.5 tbsp fresh lemon juice (to balance sweetness)
- 1.5 tsp orange zest (optional, for extra flavor)
- 'Garnish: Candied orange peel, fresh berries, or mint (optional)'
instructions:
- Heat the Cream & Sugar
- In a saucepan over medium heat, combine the heavy cream and sugar.
- Stir gently until the sugar dissolves and the mixture reaches a gentle simmer (not
  a rolling boil).
- Let it simmer for 3 minutes, stirring occasionally.
- Add Citrus Juices
- Remove the saucepan from heat and stir in the orange juice, lemon juice, and orange
  zest.
- The mixture will start to thicken slightly.
- Strain & Pour
- For a silky texture, strain the mixture through a fine sieve into a jug.
- Then, divide it into 6 serving glasses or ramekins.
- Chill
- Let the possets cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 3-4 hours
  (or overnight) until fully set.
- Serve & Garnish
- Serve chilled, garnished with candied orange peel, fresh berries, or mint.
