title: Laal Maas
slug: laal-maas
description: A fiery Rajasthani mutton curry made with Mathania chillies, aromatic
  spices, and yogurt. This slow-cooked dish features tender mutton infused with complex
  flavors from whole spices and a vibrant red chilli paste.
source: notes
rewritten: true
category:
- Main Course
cuisine: Indian
prepTime: 1 hour 15 min
cookTime: 50 min
totalTime: 2 hours 5 min
servings: '4'
ingredients:
- 1 kg mutton, curry cuts
- 2 tbsp ginger garlic paste (70% garlic + 30% ginger)
- ¼ cup ghee
- 2-3 cloves
- 10 black peppercorns
- 2 small cardamom pods
- 1 black cardamom pod
- 250 gms onion, sliced
- Salt to taste
- 20 gms Mathania chilli paste (made from soaked chillies)
- 200 gms yogurt, beaten
- 1½ tbsp coriander powder
- ½ tsp red chilli powder
- ½ tsp cumin powder
- 2 tbsp mustard oil
instructions:
- In a mixing bowl, combine mutton and ginger garlic paste. Marinate well and set
  aside for 1 hour.
- In a large sauce pot, heat ghee over medium heat. Add cloves, black peppercorns,
  small cardamom, and black cardamom. Let the spices splutter for about 1 minute.
- Add sliced onions and sauté until light brown, about 4-5 minutes.
- Add the marinated mutton and sauté for 4-5 minutes, stirring well.
- Add Mathania chilli paste and sauté for 8-10 minutes to remove any rawness from
  the chilies. Add salt to taste and mix well.
- Cover the pot and cook on low flame for 10-12 minutes.
- In a separate bowl, whisk together yogurt, coriander powder, red chilli powder,
  cumin powder, and mustard oil until well combined.
- Pour the yogurt mixture into the pot and stir thoroughly. Cover with the lid and
  cook on low flame for 30 minutes until the mutton is tender.
- Garnish with fresh coriander sprigs and serve hot with bajra roti.
notes: The ginger garlic paste ratio is important—use 70% garlic and 30% ginger for
  authentic flavor. Soaking the Mathania chillies before making paste helps develop
  deeper flavor. Cooking on low flame is essential to ensure the mutton becomes tender
  without the gravy reducing too quickly.
